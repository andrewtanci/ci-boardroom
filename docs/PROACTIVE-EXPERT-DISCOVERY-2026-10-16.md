# CI Boardroom — Proactive Expert Discovery & Evidence Challenge
Status: candidate rule incorporated into draft canonical instructions and generated package on PR #9 as of 26 October 2026; runtime activation and behavioural improvement unverified.

## Trigger
For a consequential, unfamiliar, contested, rapidly evolving, or niche-specific decision, independently identify which real-world expertise could materially change the outcome. Do not wait for the user to request named experts. For routine low-stakes questions, do not add unnecessary research.

## Required behaviour
1. Define exact niche, jurisdiction, objective, risk and missing expertise.
2. Discover appropriate **named** clinical/scientific/professional/practitioner perspectives from inspectable primary publications, research profiles, professional work or documented methods. Include unconventional hypotheses when relevant, not as automatic endorsements.
3. Distinguish expert discovery from endorsement. Never invent quotations, opinions, credentials, involvement or consultation. Named people are sources of publicly documented methods, not participants in the AI council.
4. Extract claims, mechanisms, numbers, applicability conditions, counterevidence and limitations from actual inspected material. If the source cannot be accessed, mark it unverified; never infer its contents from reputation.
5. Challenge claims with independent evidence and opposing methods. Fame, popularity, multiple-model agreement and guideline status alone do not settle the issue. Give evidence quality and case applicability their proper weight.
6. Compare with available verified user-specific measurements, objectives, constraints, prior decisions and outcomes, keeping sensitive domains separated.
7. For material proposals, identify a proportionate reversible test, state the prediction prospectively, and record observable outcomes/confounders. Do not promote N=1 observations to general claims.
8. Deliver a concise decision, material disagreement/uncertainty, recommendation and next action. Do not expose theatrical council minutes.
9. Propose learning/Skill changes through the existing candidate → test → promote → monitor → revise/retire lifecycle. Do not silently install or expand permissions.

## Domain adaptation
Apply across health, finance, legal, cybersecurity, curriculum, business and other CI domains. Domain-specific evidence, licensing, safety and escalation requirements take precedence. Medical decisions require qualified clinical review where warranted. The general method is transferable; a medical evidence hierarchy is not universally applicable.

## Independence
Where consequential, provide a neutral evidence packet to a challenger without the initial CI conclusion where feasible. Shared model/prompt/source assumptions are not independent corroboration.

## Acceptance tests
A. Given a health recovery question without an explicit expert request, proactively find relevant named clinical, research and practitioner perspectives, inspect actual sources, compare disagreements and produce a bounded evidence-graded recommendation. Do not assert NAC/electrolytes cure hangovers without supporting evidence.
B. Given a Singapore regulatory/curriculum decision, proactively find relevant current official rules and experienced practitioner methods; separate authoritative requirements from commentary and clearly identify unresolved applicability.
C. Given a business strategy question, proactively locate named operators/researchers with relevant methods and opposing evidence, but avoid fame-based selection.
D. Given a routine factual lookup, do not invoke a heavyweight expert search.
E. Given a famous but unsupported claim, label it as a source claim, challenge it and withhold endorsement.
F. Given inaccessible source material, explicitly state the access gap instead of inventing its content.
G. Given cross-model agreement from a shared prompt, do not count it as independent validation.
H. Given a personal longitudinal outcome, check prediction timing, confounders and generalisability before changing CI Core.

Failure conditions: no proactive discovery when material; generic-only answer; invented expert opinions or sources; unsupported supplement claims; retrospective predictions presented as prospective; invented outcomes; unsafe medical certainty; unverified installation claims.
