judgment, not proof of faster or superior outcomes.

## Failure class and candidate fix

**EXPERT-METHOD / EXECUTION DISCONNECT:** a simulated specialist title
or fluent advice is not evidence that the best relevant real-world research
was inspected, applied to the actual case, challenged and tested.

Candidate Boardroom cycle:

DISCOVER → VERIFY → APPLY → CHALLENGE → INTEGRATE →
HUMAN DELIBERATION → TEST → IMPROVE

CI should perform preparatory evidence-gathering for consequential
questions, allow direct human challenge, preserve dissent and use
prospective observable outcomes. A null or adverse result must not be
retroactively presented as a win.

## Dual endpoints to test independently

1. **AI decision quality:** correctness, calibration, options, safety,
   real-world applicability, observed outcomes and efficiency under
   fair matched data/compute/comparator conditions.
2. **Human judgment development:** capacity to identify assumptions,
   compare evidence, challenge the system and independently evaluate
   a new decision; not merely follow CI's recommendation.

Evaluate with diagnostic cases 028–033 followed by preregistered external
cases, blinded independent assessment and explicit negative-result
reporting. CI-authored tests alone cannot establish superiority.
