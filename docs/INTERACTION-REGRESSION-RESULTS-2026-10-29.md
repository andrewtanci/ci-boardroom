## Controls and limits

- Nine fresh native contexts: one case per condition, no inherited parent
  history, earlier answers, external research or rubric in tested sessions.
  Both CI conditions used complete text with retained sequential-read traces.
- Each case's answers were randomly anonymized before a separate fresh AI
  grader scored them. The condition mapping was withheld; no explicit branding
  leak was observed, but stylistic inference cannot be excluded.
- The same native host defaults were requested. Resolved model/version,
  reasoning/sampling settings, full hidden instructions, tokens and monetary
  cost were unavailable; equality cannot be independently certified.
- Three related cases, designed after an observed weakness, with one answer
  per cell are a narrow diagnostic. Explicit cues and ceiling scores limit
  discrimination. Separate contexts are not independent-model statistical
  evidence. A tie does not prove equivalence, broad safety or business outcomes.

This matrix did not test the ZIP in a live client or install/discover a Skill in
ChatGPT or Claude. The earlier rc.1 checkpoint trial tested two native loading
routes with the corrupted checkpoint explicitly announced and the restored file
re-supplied. It demonstrated cued conflict handling and reload, not unnoticed
corruption discovery or assistant-executed Git rollback. These observations are
separate from the 17 engineering/recovery tests and are not a new rc.2
installation or continuity pass. Real experts did not participate.
