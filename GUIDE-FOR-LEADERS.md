# Guide For Leaders

**Expert council support at your fingertips, without losing yourself or your authority.**

AI is now doing real work, not just answering questions.

It can draft messages, compare options, organise documents, analyse data, prepare checklists, write letters, summarise issues, and move work forward faster than most teams can manually coordinate.

The goal is not to slow AI down.

The goal is to get better work from AI while preserving human authority.

Core lines:

> Delegate the work. Do not delegate the authority.

> AI can do the work. AI cannot take the authority.

## The CI Ladder

CI Kernel is user-carried continuity.

Public wording: CI Kernel is a copy-paste continuation tool that stops your AI work from resetting.

The user generates and pastes a portable Continuation Block into the next AI session. This is not automatic background memory, hidden background storage, or platform-level memory.

CI Boardroom gives better work.

CI Authority Drift Review finds red flags.

CI Human Authority Proof creates evidence.

CI Governance Check verifies against current sources without giving legal or regulatory finality.

## How CI Boardroom Works

CI Boardroom forms the right expert council for the topic, runs the TNT drill internally, and returns the best usable output first.

The user does not see a long council transcript by default.

For serious decisions, optional Cross-Examination can create a privacy-safe hand-off pack for a genuinely different second AI perspective.

## Why CI Boardroom Matters

CI Boardroom helps leaders separate work execution from decision authority.

AI may draft, compare, organise, analyse, and prepare.

AI must not send, sign, file, pay, delete, approve or reject people, or make binding commitments without human authority.

Domain alone is not the trigger.

Consequential action is the trigger.

A low-risk finance draft may be fine for AI to prepare. A binding payment, regulatory filing, employee warning, rejected applicant, deleted record, signed contract, or customer commitment needs human authority.

Source Integrity remains mandatory. AI output is not fact by default. If a claim is factual, legal, policy, financial, regulatory, compliance, medical, country-specific, or current, it must be checked against appropriate sources before action.

Humans must remain able to challenge, override, pause, reverse, and explain decisions.

## Work Versus Authority

| Situation | AI can do | Human must own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft bank escalation email | draft the email | decide whether to send |
| Employment warning letter | prepare wording | verify facts and approve sending |
| Grant claim | prepare checklist | confirm eligibility and submit |
| AI candidate ranking | analyse red flags | decide whether workflow is acceptable |
| Contract review | identify issues and draft clauses | approve final wording and signing |

## Practical Rule

Let AI carry the work.

Keep humans in charge of consequence.

If the action affects money, people, customers, contracts, records, reputation, rights, safety, access, or essential services, name the human decision-owner before action.

If facts are uncertain, pause and verify.

If ownership is unclear, stop and escalate.

## Leadership Promise

AI can do the work.

AI cannot take the authority.
